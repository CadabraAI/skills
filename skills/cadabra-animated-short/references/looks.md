# Looks

Each look has three lines. LOOK opens the prompt, CRAFT follows the STYLE
line, MOTION goes into the take only. Use them word for word, and only the
lines of the one look the user chose: mixing words from two looks makes the
picture flicker between them. Add the user's own materials and colours to
LOOK; never name a studio, a film or an artist.

---

## 3D animated feature

LOOK: a finished 3D animated feature film
CRAFT: appealing exaggerated shape language with a strong readable silhouette; soft subsurface scattering in skin, fur and ears; individually groomed fur or hair with clumping; materials with true roughness, from glossy eyes to matte cloth; one motivated key light, a soft fill and a separating rim; atmospheric depth haze; shallow depth of field on the face.
MOTION: every move eases in and out; clear anticipation before each action and overlapping follow-through after it; squash and stretch with weight on contact; fur, ears and cloth settle a beat after the body stops; held poses keep a breathing micro-motion.

## Stop-motion clay

LOOK: a handmade stop-motion clay animation on a miniature set
CRAFT: modelling clay with visible thumbprints and tool marks; a slight wobble of the surface from frame to frame; real miniature props with true scale texture; practical tiny lamps as the only light, casting real dimensional shadows; a shallow focus that shows the small size of the set.
MOTION: a slight frame-to-frame jitter in the clay; poses hit and hold with a small settle; mouths and eyelids change in clear replacement steps; fast actions read as a few strong poses, not a blur.

## Hand-drawn 2D

LOOK: hand-drawn 2D animation
CRAFT: confident ink lines of one fixed weight, slightly heavier on the outer contour; flat painted colour with one soft shadow tone; painted backgrounds with visible brush texture; clean shape design that reads as a silhouette.
MOTION: movement follows curved arcs; eases in and out of every key pose; smear frames on fast turns; a short hold on the extreme pose for impact; big comic takes with stretch before the snap back; secondary motion in hair, tail and cloth.

## Watercolour storybook

LOOK: a painted watercolour storybook brought to life
CRAFT: transparent watercolour washes with pigment blooms and soft wet edges; visible paper grain; loose pencil lines under the paint; a restrained palette where white paper is the brightest light.
MOTION: gentle eased movement; washes bloom and settle as things appear; characters move as painted shapes with soft deforming edges; backgrounds drift in slow layered parallax.

## Paper cut-out

LOOK: layered paper cut-out animation
CRAFT: hand-cut and torn paper edges with visible fibres; flat coloured card with subtle texture; real cast shadows between layers at different depths; a shallow stage like a lit theatre box.
MOTION: layers slide over each other with offset shadows; parts hinge at pinned joints; turns happen as a flip to a new cut-out; movement keeps a handmade stepped rhythm.

## Felt and wool puppets

LOOK: felt and knitted wool puppet animation on a handmade set
CRAFT: fuzzy wool fibres catching a soft rim light; visible stitched seams; felt with real thickness; bead or button eyes with a bright catchlight; soft warm practical lights.
MOTION: soft bouncy movement with a small squash on every landing; fibres shimmer slightly as the puppet moves; ears and limbs flop and settle after each move.

## Neon night city

LOOK: neon night-city animation
CRAFT: deep blue darkness with saturated magenta and cyan neon as the only light sources; glowing tubes with a soft bloom; strong graphic silhouettes against the glow; fine haze in the air catching the beams.
MOTION: sharp snappy timing with strong holds; light pulses and flickers on the beat of the action; camera moves glide and stop clean.

## Ink and wash

LOOK: ink-and-wash animation
CRAFT: black brush ink on cream paper; loose expressive strokes with dry-brush texture; one single accent colour owned by the story's key prop; large areas of empty paper as space.
MOTION: strokes flow into each new pose; fast actions leave a dry-brush trail; long held stillness broken by one sudden stroke.
